# Character models: Quaternius (CC0 1.0)

All models in this folder are by **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com) from the
"Ultimate Modular Men" / "Ultimate Modular Women" packs and are released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They were downloaded as glTF binaries from the official CC0 mirror on poly.pizza
(https://poly.pizza/u/Quaternius). No attribution is required; this note is kept for provenance.
